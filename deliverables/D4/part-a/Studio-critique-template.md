## Practical review sequence

1. Ask the reviewer to identify the stage and responsible office of #E-031, then compare it with #E-024. Record confusion over On time, Overdue, expected review date or last update.
2. Show the Refused example. Ask the reviewer to explain its reason and likely next step in their own words; do not prompt the explanation.
3. Show Changes needed and correction. Inspect whether the reason remains visible and whether the editable fields match what is requested.
4. Trigger W7 with date and venue empty. Inspect summary placement, retained input, field messages and the recovery path to W8.
5. Try the empty dashboard and New Request. Check whether the action label and required information are understandable without a tour.
6. Inspect keyboard focus, non-colour statuses, narrow layouts and Help. Record accessibility observations in the separate audit log.
7. Agree a small prioritized change list, assign owners and retain deferred concerns. Do not convert critique comments into measured usability success rates.
