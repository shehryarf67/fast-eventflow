Status-first Approval Portal — wireframe flow

Core task: find out where a submitted event request stands, and act on what you find.

Deliberately low fidelity. Grayscale, boxes and labels. No brand colour, no illustration, no final typography. Status is carried by text and border weight, never by colour alone, which is the same constraint requirement R5 imposes on the built version.

Navigation additions for Part B

W1 ↔ W3 / W4 / W5. W4 → W5 is a simulated staff update. W5 → W6 → W7 → W6 → W8. Every detail returns to W1. W10 Help is available from every screen.

Dates in the original sketches are illustrative October snapshots. Part B uses a fixed 4 November 2026 clock. IA is the team-proposed hub-and-spoke structure, grounded in D2 synthesis.

Annotation — W1

Role in flow
Entry screen and hub. Every request the lead owns, in one list, answering R1 without contacting anyone.
Visual hierarchy
Request title heaviest, status chip second by border weight and position, metadata smallest and greyest. The reader's first fixation lands on title plus status.
Gestalt — proximity
Title, stage bar and office line sit inside one bordered region with tight internal spacing and a wide gap between cards, so each request reads as one object rather than six loose lines.
Non-colour status
On time is a 1.5px border, Overdue a 3px border plus the word and elapsed days. Shape and text, not hue.

Annotation — W7

Role in flow
The required alternate path. Submission is attempted with two required fields empty.
Error behaviour
A summary at the top states what did not happen and that the draft survives, then each field carries its own specific message. Both are needed: the summary for someone who submitted and looked away, the inline message for someone fixing the field.
Messages are actionable
Each says what to do, and the venue error volunteers what is actually free, so the lead is not sent back to guess.
Non-colour error marking
Thicker border on the field, a bordered exclamation mark, and bold message text. No red.
Audit point
In the built version the summary takes focus and is announced; the team walkthrough form is supplied for recording actual observations.
