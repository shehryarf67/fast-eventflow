D08 - Shmeezki & Mahid · D4 · Team-proposed navigation activity

Facilitator instructions

Print the cards, cut along the borders and shuffle. Show only card labels; proposed navigation categories are intentionally omitted. Record consent and anonymous role categories. Allow about twenty minutes and retain independent placements before group discussion.

Read aloud

These cards describe things an event-request portal might let you do. Group things where you would expect to find them together. Make as many or as few groups as you need. Name each group. Put confusing cards aside or rename them. There are no correct answers; explain the choices that matter to you.

Use the separate results form and CSV. Ask where the person would look for an overdue request, a refusal reason, correction and new submission. Compare proposed labels only after the open sort.
