# Research and implementation traceability

## Source basis

D1A establishes assumption A1 and the research question about how leads find out a request's status and act during silence. D1B records seven interviews and discloses a student-weighted sample: six society leads, one mentor and no One Stop or student-affairs officials. D2 defines 35 evidence units, the affinity synthesis, Hira, the point of view and five measurable requirements. The supplied wall photographs corroborate that earlier synthesis; they are not a D4 feature sort or a new study. No participant identities, real society names or raw research photographs are republished in the prototype.

D3 selects the status-first portal and explains the additions of SF1 submission checking, AM5 reasoned decisions and MS5 overdue escalation. This is the mapping used in the build. The current project specification treats the selected concept as the implementation baseline; D3's earlier “team to confirm” wording is not evidence of a later meeting.

The authoritative D4 brief requires a card-sort-derived IA and the team's own accessibility observations. This package supplies a proposed IA, activity pack and recording forms; prepared forms are not substituted for activity records. It also requires original team narration, captions and a live instructor checkpoint, which cannot be inferred from an automated build.
